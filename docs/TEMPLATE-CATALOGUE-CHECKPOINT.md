The browser persists the original command, body and idempotency key in its workspace/actor scope before sending. Interrupted or malformed responses, auth errors and uncertain outcomes retain that identity through reload and navigation. Changed inputs cannot replace a pending command. Shared controls synchronously coordinate the pending receipt; stale workspace completions cannot navigate or acknowledge work in another scope. Storage failure/corruption fails closed, with32 scoped slots. Web Locks provide available cross-tab exclusion; fallback is same-document coordination and does not establish exhaustive cross-tab atomicity.

Deliberate dismissal is limited to proven nonexecution: TEMPLATE_ARCHIVED409 or VERSION_MISMATCH412 on the original archive/remix, and ASSET_NOT_READY409 on the original remix only. Keyed successful historical receipts precede these callbacks; asset refusal throws inside the enclosing tenant transaction, rolling back provisional child work without a success receipt. Generic4xx, wrong operation/status, unknown results and authentication errors cannot receive this classification. The exact rejected receipt persists until the person explicitly dismisses it. Asset checks are not relaxed.
